### Unexpired certification

#### Option 1: Pass the Terraform Infrastructure Engineer Professional exam starting 6 months before expiration

- Extend your Professional credentials' expiration date

#### Option 2: Pass either the AWS or Azure version of the Terraform Authoring and Operations Advanced exam

- Extend your Professional credentials' expiration date
- Earn a new advanced-level badge and certificate

### Expired certification

- Retake the exam at any time
- Passing gives you a new set of credentials with a new expiration date

Learn more about recertification in our [Knowledge base](https://hashicorp-certifications.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9677396620941).
